# 01 · Project charter

## Product promise

**Understand India's water conditions. Explore your place. Inspect the evidence.**

Build a lasting public-interest publication, not a campaign page designed to prove one alarming thesis. It begins with the 2026 monsoon question but must serve readers through the northeast monsoon, the following dry season, recovery and subsequent years. Dates are data, not strings hardcoded to 2026.

A first-time reader should be able to answer, without specialist knowledge:

1. What is happening, where, and as of when?
2. Is the problem rainfall, stored water, groundwater, water quality, access, or several together?
3. How unusual is it compared with a genuinely comparable past?
4. What could follow, what would reduce the risk, and what is not yet knowable?
5. What are authorities and other institutions actually doing, and where can I verify it?
6. What information is useful to my household, community, work or reporting?

## Audiences

General readers on mobile; people checking a district or city; journalists seeking reproducible charts and local leads; researchers checking methods; public officials checking the record. Serve a 30-second reader, a five-minute reader and a deep researcher through progressive disclosure, not three duplicated sites.

## Geographic scope

India-wide landing and state/UT index, with district detail only at the resolution supported by each metric. Basin and city-service-area views are separate from administrative geography. Include northeast India, Himalayan regions, island territories, central India and western drylands rather than extrapolating the south to the entire country. Investigate each region's relevant seasons, storage systems and demand structure.

Discover the current administrative inventory from an authoritative directory. Version it. A district with rainfall data but no water-supply data must not look as though its taps have been assessed.

## Editorial form

A concise national briefing, an exploratory atlas, durable explainers, evidence-backed investigations, a latest-developments desk, a conditional outlook, and an exportable journalist desk. Do not dump hundreds of loosely related articles on one page. Select a few meaningful, contextualized changes and give readers paths to detail.

The lead feature should feel like a major visual news investigation: a striking dated opening, scenes that change rhythm as the reader moves, source-linked charts and diagrams, reader-controlled comparisons, and a clear route from observed weather to storage, official action, lived experience, markets and the next decision point. Make a 30-second reader understand the most surprising verified finding on a phone's first screen. Give a five-minute reader meaningful controls rather than a sequence of decorative cards. Motion must reveal a real change of view or an exact value; it must not imply unobserved intermediate measurements.

Show dated firsthand voices and political statements when a verifiable published record exists, with speaker, outlet/document and context. Use original typographic treatments of linked coverage; do not reproduce article screenshots, publisher photos or artwork without a reusable licence or permission. Investigate claims of a media blackout against actual coverage rather than treating them as a premise. Describe market exposure, price movements and attributed causes as separate evidentiary steps.

## What success looks like

- One comprehensible current national picture that shows regional disagreement and data gaps.
- Every major number inspectable in two interactions, with source, date, unit, normal/denominator and method.
- Maps with an accessible table alternative and shareable filters.
- Historical comparisons computed from stable geographies/definitions, with reconstruction eras visually separated.
- Honest population/demand analysis that does not equate rainfall with available water.
- Concrete reporting on public response, livelihoods, economy, distributional effects and realistic protective actions.
- A maintainable refresh pipeline, not a page that must be rewritten by hand every week.
- A static build that remains readable during traffic spikes and does not charge per visitor.
- A visually memorable mobile feature with light and dark themes, legible reduced-motion and no-JavaScript reading paths, and interaction that preserves exact dates, source identity and uncertainty.

## Editorial stance

Be serious without being apocalyptic. There may be severe local crises without a uniform national crisis. An officially declared drought and the publication's analysis are different labels. Improvement, adaptation and uncertainty deserve visible treatment, not a hidden caveat box.

Do not make political recommendations, rank governments or parties, speculate about motives, or turn the response tracker into an electoral tool. Report documented actions, spending stages and measured outcomes with attribution and limitations. An evidence gap is not proof of inaction.

## Authority and transparency

Identify the real publisher and actual contributors only after confirmation. Clearly disclose AI-assisted research and development, the review process, limitations, corrections and contact method. Never invent a newsroom, independent experts, field visits or institutional endorsement. Do not use government logos or emergency-service styling that implies an official service.

## Scope discipline

The final destination is broad; the implementation is phased. Build one complete verified vertical slice, then extend the same contracts. Do not generate 700 thin district pages to simulate completeness. A useful, evidence-rich national release with honest local gaps is better than a giant misleading atlas.

No accounts, comments, paywall, chatbot, user-generated incident map, payment flow or native app in launch scope. No unnecessary Kubernetes, vector database, custom CMS, scraping-as-a-service or paid tile platform.

## Publication identity
India Water Watch is a working title, not a claim of trademark or domain availability. Before public launch, check naming conflicts and configure the real publisher identity/contact with authorization. Do not imply official affiliation, buy a domain or invent an editorial staff.
