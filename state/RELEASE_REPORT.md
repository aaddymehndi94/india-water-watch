The approved snapshot contains **38 source-linked claims, 14 numeric observations, 30 evidence records and 44 geographies**. Rainfall has two cumulative cutoffs; CWC monitored storage is dated 10 September and the Bengaluru DCA prices have separate 2025 and 2026 dates. `state/COVERAGE_AND_FRESHNESS.md` records all watermarks and gaps. Source-chain, arithmetic, response-stage and rights reviews are in `reporting/g14_independent_source_review.md` and `reporting/g14_postpromotion_source_review.md`. These are independent **agent** reviews, not field reporting, a legal opinion or human photo clearance.

No nationwide household-shortage count, matched state/district service measure, 2026 stock attribution, delivered Karnataka drought relief, or 2027 monsoon forecast is verified. The site-authored AI Author scenario is qualitative and conditional, with its older 23 September input date kept visible; it is not a probabilistic forecast. The national rainfall departure is a weather measurement, not proof of an India-wide water crisis or universal shortage.
