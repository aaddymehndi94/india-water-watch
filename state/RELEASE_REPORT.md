## What changed

A full [design and editorial review](../reporting/g15_design_review.md) found that the earlier cover carried real evidence but the chapter rhythm was too repetitive and the most useful interactive comparison came too late. G15 places a large rainfall scene directly after the first screen. It uses the separately inspected IMD 1 June–24 September national totals on a **shared zero baseline: 723.6 mm recorded, 843.2 mm same-date normal, 119.6 mm gap and −14.2% departure**. The reader can switch between totals and the gap; this changes the view, not the source or observation period. The completed-June comparator remains in a disclosure because its window and normal differ. The scene is readable without JavaScript and labels what rainfall cannot say about taps, reservoirs or crops.

A new three-perspective interaction gives a farmer, a flood-affected resident and a chief minister's reported relief pledge separate, dated space. P Narsi Reddy's Telangana account was reported by *The New Indian Express* on 16 September; Geeta Devi's Bihar flood account by *Mongabay India* on 18 September; Devendra Fadnavis's 23 September pledge was reported by *The Indian Express* on 24 September. The passages are brief, attributed and linked; these are not India Water Watch interviews or evidence of prevalence or aid delivered. All three remain readable without JavaScript. News article images, logos and screenshots are excluded without reuse rights; the previously published licensed **July** Nashik flood photograph remains distinctly dated.

The response page now opens with the contrast between **177 declared Karnataka drought-affected taluks** and **₹1,852.65 crore requested** in a 23 September interim-assistance letter. Approval, transfer, delivery and outcomes remain unverified. The existing six-stage response tracker shows the differences. The data-status page displays an audit of the IMD district interface: **722 numeric rows dated 24 September, eight older numeric rows and 31 explicit No Data rows among 761 unique map areas**. Two displayed district departures differ materially from arithmetic on the displayed millimetres. This is a source-page quality graphic, not a district water map or a set of approved observations. The impacts chapter adds MoSPI's provisional **August 2026 CFPI 5.95% year-on-year**, issued 14 September, as food-price context without attributing it to rainfall, drought or stock moves.
