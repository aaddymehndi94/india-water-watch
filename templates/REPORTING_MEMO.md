# Reporting memo
Beat / reporter role / snapshot / date:
## Reader question and provisional finding
Write the finding only after investigation. A neutral or improving result is valid.
## Evidence
For each assertion: claim ID, exact source/product, locator, observation period, geography, derivation, and what the source cannot show.
## History and comparison
Eligible years/regions, boundary/normal changes, denominator, missingness and excluded analogues.
## Counterevidence and alternative explanations
What would weaken the proposed story? Distinguish absence of evidence from evidence of absence.
## People and distribution
Who is exposed, who is buffered, whose experience is not in the data? Do not invent people or interviews.
## Institutions and actions
Authority/jurisdiction; documented announcement; implementation evidence; outcome evidence; unresolved questions. No rankings or presumed motives.
## Visual opportunities
Chart/map question, encoding, source IDs, table fallback, and an honest empty state.
## Practical information
Source-dated official guidance; applicability; safety and human review needs.
## Publication decision
Publish / attribute / investigate / withhold. Reviewer, exact source packet and unresolved human-reporting work.
