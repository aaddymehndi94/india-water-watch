The source PDFs were read locally from the original CGWB URLs and key pages visually checked. Raw PDFs remain in `/tmp` and are not public artifacts.

## January 2026 well-depth document

- **Original link and locator:** The [CGWB Ground Water Level Monitoring page](https://cgwb.gov.in/en/ground-water-level-monitoring) links “January ground water level data 2026” to [`/wl_data/January_2026.pdf`](https://cgwb.gov.in/wl_data/January_2026.pdf). Direct diagnostic GET returned `application/pdf`, 2,059,803 bytes, **447 PDF pages**. SHA-256: `b4a23c8073b3b0b61c242a865930eff2c13c3e8ff1433a7a6a7990e27c037beb`. Page 1 was visually checked. The repeated table headings say `DEPTH TO WATER LEVEL (in mbgl) of unconfined data Pre-Monsoon January - 2026` and columns `STATE_UT`, `DISTRICT`, `BLOCK`, `VILLAGE`, `LATITUDE`, `LONGITUDE`, `DATE`, `DTWL`. `mbgl` means metres below ground level; a larger positive number indicates a deeper water level at that observation, not a larger stored volume.
- **Date quality flag:** Text extraction yielded **16,952 date cells**, all reading **`10-01-2026`**, across 447 pages. Page 1 visibly has the same date on every row inspected. The uniform date may be a reporting/formatting convention or a source error; it must **not** be presented as independently verified measurement dates for 16,952 wells. The heading's “Pre-Monsoon January” wording is internally inconsistent. It is safe to identify the PDF as CGWB's *January 2026 labeled* table; do not describe it as a standard pre-monsoon survey or compare with a May pre-monsoon series until CGWB's meaning is resolved.
- **Coverage quality flag:** The file lists locations in multiple states/UTs, but no stable well identifier or network inclusion/exclusion description was established in the document. Some rows share place names/coordinates yet have different depths. Count of extracted date cells is **not** a verified count of unique wells. It cannot support a national mean depth, a district average, a water-storage change, or a September 2026 groundwater condition without well matching, network coverage, aquifer, sampling design and comparable historical observations.

**Proposed narrow public copy, if the dataset is listed at all:** “CGWB provides a table labeled **January 2026** of depth-to-water-level entries, in metres below ground level. The published PDF does not yet provide enough verified well identity and date context for this site to calculate a national groundwater trend.” Link to the [PDF](https://cgwb.gov.in/wl_data/January_2026.pdf) and describe the metadata inconsistency in a methodology note. The stronger release choice is a source-status entry rather than a chart.

## Access, rights and reproducible handling

Normal `requests` and `curl` HTTPS access to `cgwb.gov.in` in this environment failed with **`CERTIFICATE_VERIFY_FAILED: unable to get local issuer certificate`** / curl exit 60. A **diagnostic** `requests.get(..., verify=False)` could download both official-domain PDFs and the catalog page; this is not acceptable as an unattended production adapter and does not prove the certificate chain. Browser/web fetches of the catalog, monitoring page and well PDF separately timed out or returned 502. The locally inspected content has exact endpoint URLs and hashes above, but transport-authentication uncertainty must stay visible until a trusted chain/official mirror is available.

Legitimate manual-import route: open the CGWB catalog and document link in a browser that validates the certificate, or obtain the same original file from an authenticated CGWB/NWIC mirror without bypassing access controls. Save it in ignored local raw-source storage; record exact URL, retrieval time, PDF size and SHA-256, provider title/version, and any publication date shown. Visually check PDF p. 21 and the method pages against parsed cells; require a second person or independent script to confirm the ratio. If trusted access remains unavailable, retain the current source-status report and hold the public figures. Do not disable TLS validation in scheduled ingestion and do not auto-promote a replacement PDF merely because the URL is unchanged. The monitoring page links a [CGWB data dissemination policy](https://cgwb.gov.in/cgwbpnm/public/uploads/documents/16868329231817838139file.pdf); the precise rights for bulk table or PDF republication have not been cleared. Public output should link to original PDFs and use brief attributed facts, while rights review addresses any bulk reuse.
