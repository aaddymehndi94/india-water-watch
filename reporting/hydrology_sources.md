# Hydrology source inspection — 24 September 2026 (IST)

Scope: official observed rainfall, reservoir live storage, groundwater resource assessment and groundwater well depth. This is a source audit for the editor/adapter team, not permission to publish every figure below. Retrieval checks occurred on 24 September 2026 IST. Source pages can revise in place; an adapter must retain retrieval time, raw hash, provider date and prior versions separately.

## 1. IMD current rainfall: usable, with an important coverage caveat

- **Publisher/exact endpoints:** India Meteorological Department (IMD), [districts](https://mausam.imd.gov.in/responsive/rainfallinformation.php?msg=C), [states and UTs](https://mausam.imd.gov.in/responsive/rainfallinformation_state.php?msg=C), [meteorological subdivisions](https://mausam.imd.gov.in/responsive/rainfallinformation_msd.php?msg=C). `msg=D/W/M/C` selects daily/weekly/monthly/cumulative. Each endpoint returned HTTP 200 HTML in a direct read on 24 September 2026; page text and embedded observations were inspected, not just the home page.
- **Exact locator:** Inline JavaScript `AmCharts.makeChart("chartdiv", {dataProvider: {areas: [...]}})`; each area has `id`, `title`, `info`, `balloonText`. The `balloonText` contains actual rainfall, normal rainfall, and rounded departure; the district records also contain `Date : 2026-09-23`. Parse the JSON array, then parse the fields; never scrape a map colour as a quantity. Validate the record count, unique IDs, numeric values, missing values and date before committing a new snapshot. A 24 September retrieval yielded **761 district map records, 31 explicitly `No Data`**; the state page includes regional/country summary entries among its 41 map areas, so 41 is not a count of states or UTs. It also marks three regional summary entries `No Data`; exclude these from geographic aggregation.
- **Observed period:** The cumulative radio label on the state/subdivision pages reads `01-06-2026 To 23-09-2026`. The daily page states rainfall was recorded **08:30 IST 22 September to 08:30 IST 23 September 2026**. The cumulative page does not spell out its end hour, so preserve IMD's dated cutoff and do not invent a timestamp for the cumulative measurement. The source's all-India country row on the state page says **713.3 mm actual, 837.7 mm normal, −15% rounded departure** for the cumulative period. This is an observed precipitation departure, not a shortage, drought declaration, or usable-water estimate. The calculation from displayed rounded mm is about −14.85%, consistent with the source's rounded −15%; a downstream transform should use source precision or mark rounding.
- **Normal/baseline:** The current interactive page does not itself state the normal reference years. IMD's [2022 annual report](https://mausam.imd.gov.in/imd_latest/contents/ar2022.pdf), Hydromet Services section, says CRIS adopted **1971–2020** rainfall normals in 2022; IMD's [13 April 2026 seasonal forecast](https://mausam.imd.gov.in/Forecast/mcmarq/mcmarq_data/english.pdf) states its all-India southwest monsoon LPA is based on 1971–2020. Treat this as strong contextual evidence, but verify the exact normal used in each current district/state product before labeling its baseline in public. Do not mix the current page with an earlier normal series.
- **Geography:** District map IDs are embedded and tied to IMD's linked `india_gj_2024.geojson`, whose boundary licence, disputed-border treatment and administrative alignment require separate review. The map's 761 records are not a complete verified inventory of 2026 districts. Keep 31 missing separate from measured zero, retain source names and IDs, and crosswalk to an independently versioned administrative geography. State/subdivision records have their own IDs; no name-only join.
- **Rights:** Page footer says © 2026 IMD; no blanket reuse/open licence was verified on these pages. Preserve links and brief factual extracts, do not republish their map art/GeoJSON until rights are checked. The exact historical data supply rules may differ from public page viewing.
- **Static adapter:** One offline HTTP GET per endpoint, conservative cadence, ETag/Last-Modified/hash check where supported; parser for the embedded `areas` JSON; strict count/date/missingness checks; record raw content hash and source URL; output normalized own tables with `actual_mm`, `normal_mm`, source displayed departure, recomputed departure, period and status. A source changing its DOM or returning only a map image fails closed and leaves the last verified dated snapshot visible. For state exploration, use the state endpoint's original aggregate, not a mean of district percentages.

### Cross-check: IMD's own national and homogeneous-region graphics

The rainfall page's `All India Rainfall Time Series` menu embeds the exact [all-India GIF](https://mausam.imd.gov.in/ClimateInformation/imdweb/DAY_WEEK/all-india.gif); `Rainfall Over Homogeneous Regions` embeds the exact [regional GIF](https://mausam.imd.gov.in/ClimateInformation/imdweb/DAY_WEEK/homo.gif). Both returned `image/gif` on 24 September 2026 and were visually inspected at original resolution. The all-India graphic labels cumulative **1 June–23 September 2026** as **713.3 mm actual vs 837.7 mm normal (1971–2020), −14.9%**; its one-decimal departure is more precise than the rounded −15% state-page country row. The regional graphic labels cumulative departure **till 23 September** as **South Peninsular India −25.7%, East & Northeast India −26.5%, Northwest India −10.5%, Central India −5.3%**, with the daily normal line explicitly labeled **1971–2020**. The images also say they are based on realtime data, so later revision is possible. Exact locator: upper-right text of each regional cumulative departure panel, and the middle/bottom panels of the all-India image. These are raster observations with no tabular numerical export on that menu; store image hashes if cited and avoid extracting intermediate daily points by pixel position. They support a dated statement about uneven rainfall, but neither graphic measures runoff, reservoir storage, groundwater, delivered water or official drought status. The national mean is not an unweighted average of the four displayed departure percentages.

## 2. IMD historical comparator: monthly series only for completed like periods

- [IMD Pune Climate Prediction and Monitoring Group, All India / regional rainfall tables](https://www.imdpune.gov.in/cmpg/Product/Rainfall_Data.html) returned HTTP 200 on 24 September 2026. The page has HTML tables with year, June, July, August, September, June–September amount in mm and percent departures; inspected table ends at **2025**. Its scope is **complete months / completed seasons**, not a daily-to-23-September series. [IMD Data Service Portal free rainfall page](https://dsp.imdpune.gov.in/home_ogd_rainfall.php) returned HTTP 200 but requires region selection; it advertises all-India monthly and seasonal rainfall in mm. [A second IMD Pune table](https://www.imdpune.gov.in/cmpg/Product/homo.html) includes all-India monthly rows and a 1971–2020 normal row. Exact table IDs and licensing need adapter validation.
- A **2026 June–23 September rank against full June–September 1901–2025 totals is invalid**. Practical launch comparator: wait for 30 September and use completed-season totals, or display the completed **June–August** 2026 months against the same months from a single consistent source if those source values are independently obtained. A same-day historical rank requires a comparable daily archive, presently **not verified accessible**. The live page's `All India Rainfall Time Series` menu points to an image GIF, not downloadable daily values. Record boundary/normal changes, year coverage and provisional status before any rank. Do not call empirical rank a recurrence probability.
- Rights on the old CMPG HTML page are not explicitly granted. Parse permitted numeric facts and link back; do not mirror tables or chart images until checked. A reproducible adapter can parse year/region/month/amount from the HTML and reject missing/duplicate years and impossible totals; label it **historical monthly**.

## 3. CWC reservoir storage: authoritative PDF structure verified; latest 2026 PDF unresolved

- **Publisher/product:** Central Water Commission (CWC) [Reservoir Level & Storage Bulletin listing](https://cwc.gov.in/en/reservoir-level-storage-bulletin) returned HTTP 200 on 24 September 2026 but the visible paginated entries inspected were older 2025 PDFs. CWC's newer [Reservoir Storage Monitoring System public dashboard](https://rsms.cwc.gov.in/frameWork/web/public-dashboard) was announced by [PIB on 3 April 2025](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2118336&lang=2&reg=48). The portal shell returned HTTP 200, but its current bulletin API/PDF link was **not established** in this audit. A 2026 PDF at [29 January 2026](https://rsms.cwc.gov.in/admin/storage/bulletins/bulletin-29-01-2026-77.pdf) was searchable and has 166 monitored reservoirs; an [18 September 2025 PDF](https://rsms.cwc.gov.in/admin/storage/bulletins/bulletin-18-09-2025-56.pdf), Table 1 p. 6, has live-capacity and current/previous/normal storage in **BCM** and a 161-reservoir cohort. These are examples of exact structures, not current 2026 observations.
- **Temporal/cohort warning:** A current 17 September 2026 figure was reported by [Informist](https://informistmedia.com/CommodityWire/59954/CWC-Data-Water-storage-in-key-reservoirs-at-131-17-billion-cubic-metres-as-of-Thursday-down-21-37-on-year) as CWC data for **178 reservoirs**, 131.17 BCM, 71% of their combined live capacity. This is a *secondary report* and does not establish an inspected primary PDF. Another derivative [Dataful page](https://dataful.in/datasets/19367/) says its weekly table runs through 17 September 2026; it reports 165 reservoirs in its source description. These potentially different cohorts must not be merged or resolved by assumption. No current CWC figure should be promoted to verified primary status until the exact dated PDF/table and inventory are captured.
- **Definitions and adapter:** Parse each bulletin's cover date, number of monitored reservoirs, reservoir identity, live capacity at full reservoir level (**BCM**, denominator), current live storage (**BCM**), corresponding prior-year same-week live storage and 10-year average normal storage. `100 * sum(current live storage) / sum(live capacity)` is a cohort fill percentage; `100 * sum(current) / sum(normal)` is a different comparison. Preserve source PDF hash, PDF page/table/row, capacity changes, reservoir additions/removals, and names/IDs; review parsed cells visually. Build both a fixed common-reservoir cohort for year comparisons and a current-system descriptive total. An official [NWIC data portal](https://www.nwdp.nwic.gov.in/en/dataset/reservoir-water-level-manual-daily-centre-water-commission_cwc) offers CSV/API resources but mixes **water levels** and **storage**; each resource must be checked before use, and a level is not automatically a stored volume.
- **Rights:** CWC/RSMS page rights and bulletin redistribution licence were not verified. Link PDFs and publish brief independently calculated summaries only after source verification. Do not mirror full PDFs publicly by default.

## 4. CGWB groundwater: 2025 resources and January 2026 depth are different indicators

- **2025 annual assessment:** Central Ground Water Board (CGWB), [National Compilation on Dynamic Ground Water Resources of India, 2025](https://cgwb.gov.in/cgwbpnm/public/publication-detail/1741); the page's [Download link](https://cgwb.gov.in/cgwbpnm/public/download/1741) served an `application/pdf` file (10,725,364 bytes) on 24 September 2026. Exact locator: printed `AT A GLANCE` immediately after contents, PDF text lines 264–270. It states **448.52 BCM total annual recharge**, **407.75 BCM annual extractable resources**, **247.22 BCM annual extraction**, **60.63% stage of extraction**. These are **2025 assessment estimates**, not 2026 measurements. The ratio 247.22 / 407.75 × 100 = 60.63% at displayed precision. State and assessment-unit tables follow; retain their unit, category and geographic definition. [CGWB methodology page](https://cgwb.gov.in/en/ground-water-resource-assessment-0) defines stage as annual extraction / annual extractable resource and links GEC-2015 and prior reports. Its access through Python failed certificate validation; diagnostic retrieval with validation disabled succeeded, so automated production use should fail closed or use a valid trust chain/manual import until fixed.
- **Well depths:** [CGWB Ground Water Level Monitoring](https://cgwb.gov.in/en/ground-water-level-monitoring) links exact [January 2026 well records PDF](https://cgwb.gov.in/wl_data/January_2026.pdf) (2,059,803 bytes) and [pre-monsoon well records 1994–2025 PDF](https://cgwb.gov.in/wl_data/Pre-monsoon_WL_1994-2025.pdf) (92,264,056 bytes, not downloaded in full). January 2026 PDF's first page/table columns are `STATE_UT`, `DISTRICT`, `BLOCK`, `VILLAGE`, `LATITUDE`, `LONGITUDE`, `DATE`, `DTWL`; heading says depth to water level in **metres below ground level (mbgl)**. It includes rows dated 10 January 2026. **Source quality flag:** PDF heading also says `Pre-Monsoon January - 2026`, an inconsistent season label. Use the observation date and page title, and ask the provider or inspect methodology before claiming a seasonal comparison. Repeated place/coordinate rows exist and may represent separate wells; do not deduplicate on names/coordinates without stable well IDs. Water-table depth is not extractable groundwater storage. The public page advertises quarterly monitoring, but the actual latest linked well observation verified here is **January 2026**, not a live September line.
- **Access/rights:** The CGWB pages and PDFs returned HTTP 200 only with TLS certificate verification disabled in this environment; the cert chain error is a real adapter blocker. Do not silently disable certificate checks in a scheduled pipeline. The page links a [hydrometeorological data dissemination policy](https://cgwb.gov.in/cgwbpnm/public/uploads/documents/16868329231817838139file.pdf); the PDF is available, but reuse terms were not conclusively extracted in this audit. Link official PDFs, keep raw PDF local, and seek rights review before publishing large derived well tables.
- **Static adapter:** A manually imported, hashed PDF is viable now. Use table-specific extraction with page/row retention, verify cell sums and a sample visually, normalize units to BCM, and separately model `assessment_year=2025` and each well's January 2026 observation date. Avoid district aggregation of sparse well depths without network/coverage analysis. No daily extrapolation.

## Release implications and next checks

1. IMD district/state/subdivision cumulative rainfall is the strongest current official 2026 observation path. The all-India 1 June–23 September departure is a **scoped rainfall fact**, not evidence that all India has a water crisis. Show 31 missing district records and avoid a historical same-day rank.
2. Resolve CWC RSMS current bulletin endpoint and retain exact 2026 PDF before publishing a national storage number; if unresolved, show last verified dated bulletin with a stale badge. Require a stable cohort before a year-on-year chart.
3. CGWB 2025 resource assessment can inform structural context; January 2026 water levels can be displayed as dated point measurements only after PDF parsing/coverage/rights checks. Neither is a September 2026 groundwater condition.
4. All three sources need source-rights review before republication of bulk tables, maps or graphics. Observation date, provider publication date (when available), retrieval date, review date and deployment date must remain separate.
