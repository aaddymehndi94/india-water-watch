## Comparability, dates and editorial presentation

- The public home page places 23 September rainfall and 10 September storage in separate dated panels, says CWC and IMD southern geographies differ, and does not infer a national water-supply outcome. The history page explicitly refuses a 2026 rank against complete seasons.
- The Karnataka story calls the 53 taluks **additional**, distinguishes declaration from relief and outcome, and the state page has a separate action record rather than treating it as a rainfall measurement.
- The source page displays the IMD forecast's **validity end** separately from observed-through dates. Karnataka's publication time is recorded at **date precision**; a midnight placeholder is not shown as a known publication hour.
- Source links are adjacent to the main numerical assertions in the eight story pages. The illustrative worsening/recovery paths in `outlook` are labelled scenarios without a probability or impact forecast. No invented quote, byline, expert endorsement, or national drought designation was found.
- Remaining uncertainty is visible: no matched current groundwater, river-flow, local service, crop-loss, or September household-impact series is approved. Missing evidence is not described as normal conditions.
