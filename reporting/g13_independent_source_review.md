### Typographic collage approval

A **source-linked original typographic card** can be built now for M01, M02, M03, M06, M07, M08 and M09, provided each carries publisher, publication date, place, evidence type, concise *newly written* paraphrase, and a link. M10 may be a link-only AP flood card, but the official MoD flood evidence is stronger for a public counterpoint. **No article screenshot, masthead imitation, newsroom logo, PTI/ANI/AP/outlet photo, interview portrait or embedded wire image has reuse clearance.** A link to the publisher's page is not a licence to display the image here. The collage must not make reported interviewees appear to be this site's interviewees.

These sources share upstream chains where figures come from IMD, KSNDMC or ministries. Multiple articles citing the same agency do not independently corroborate the measurement. Every card remains a report about a particular place and date, not a current national observation.
