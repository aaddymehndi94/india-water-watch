# Economy, access, health and accountability desk memo

Research cut-off: 24 September 2026. Status: **desk research for editorial review**, not field reporting or a finding of a 2026 water crisis. Source pages below were inspected on 24 September 2026. “Reported” refers to the source's own measurement or administrative reporting; it does not mean an independently established outcome.

**Correction, 24 September 2026:** On re-opening the full JJM 2024 national report, EA-04's earlier shorthand “76% water meeting prescribed quality standards” was found too broad. The 76% quality result is for sampled household water tested for microbiological parameters (with on-site pH); source chemical testing is separate. The report's also-76% “functionality” figure is the minimum of separately estimated quantity, quality and regularity percentages, not an observed joint household rate. EA-04 below now states these limits. See `reporting/access_public_claims.md` for the publication wording.

## Editorial answers and open questions by beat

### H — Unequal access and coping resources

The NSO survey supports a dated rural/urban service contrast, but it does not establish how wealth, caste, disability, tenancy or sex changed 2026 exposure. The full NSS unit-level data could support a careful cross-tab for dwelling tenure and access, subject to weights and disclosure rules; this memo has not computed one. Document tanker prices, storage, collection time and service hours **by named city, date and provider** before saying money buys a given buffer. Some 2018 respondents used improved sources and reported sufficient supply, which should stay visible alongside access gaps (EA-01–02).

### I — Shared limits

A dependency graphic may show **local source/aquifer → extraction and recharge → utility or private well → treatment/electricity/distribution → household/business**, with separate links to crops and food markets. Label it “possible dependencies” until local supply-chain documents are added. CGWB's resource ratio supports investigation of a shared aquifer (EA-05); it does not justify “everyone will be affected equally.” A purchased tanker or storage tank can bridge some outages, but its availability, price, source and quality need local records. No representative rich or poor household should be invented.

### J — Economy and livelihoods

Investigate **exposure → output → price/income** as separate steps. For a crop, align district sowing and irrigation, weather window, yield/output estimate or final figure, arrivals and the Price Monitoring System's market and retail/wholesale series (EA-06–07). RBI's [Annual Report 2022–23](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/AnnualReportPublications.aspx?Id=1373), chapter II, §II.3.16, attributes specific 2022 vegetable volatility to several mechanisms including excess rain, uneven monsoon and crop damage; that historical account shows why “less rain means higher price” is inadequate. It is **not** a transferable causal coefficient for 2026. No observed 2026 economy-wide water-loss estimate, migration total, income loss or mortality forecast was verified for this memo.

### K — Quality, health and ecosystems

Use EA-04 to show measured service quality **within its sample** and EA-08 for general health mechanisms. Keep a local lab test, official advisory and disease surveillance as three distinct records. Do not translate a rainfall or reservoir map into a potability label. Ecological flow, wetlands and fisheries require separate station and ecological data; none was verified for publication here.

### M — Public-information guides

Suitable *topics to research*, not ready-to-publish instructions: locate the municipality or Gram Panchayat supply schedule; check local water-quality and boil-water advisories; report a leak through the issuing utility's verified channel; document outage date/location without identifying vulnerable households; examine collective maintenance and source sustainability. Do **not** display a phone number, dosing or treatment step from this memo. A guide should link to the responsible authority for that location and show its last verification date, with human review before actionable health guidance.

### N — Accountability question bank (draft only; no outreach performed)

1. Which office owns the scheme-level supply schedule and publishes outages, delivered volume and water-quality tests? Request the dated records and definitions.
2. For a declared Har Ghar Jal village, what is the difference between connection count and tested functionality, and how were 2024 assessment villages/households selected (EA-03–04)?
3. Which aquifer or surface source serves each scheme, and what assessment unit, recharge/extraction vintage and permitted withdrawals apply (EA-05)?
4. Which district crop and market records would distinguish drought exposure from observed harvest loss and retail-price change (EA-06–07)?
5. For a claimed repair or conservation success, what was sanctioned, spent, physically completed and independently measured after completion? Request records for each stage separately.

These are questions, not allegations. Do not treat absent documents in this project as evidence that an institution failed to act. No request or right-of-reply contact has been sent.

### O — Recovery and what may be working

Connection expansion (EA-03) is a documented reported output. The 2024 functionality sample (EA-04) supplies a check on whether connections delivered service, including evidence of gaps. Neither proves a particular intervention improved health or saved time. A recovery story needs paired local observations before and after an intervention, rainfall context, source status, cost and counterevidence.

### P — Publishable gap index

- **District demand and household coping costs:** no verified comparable 2026 panel in this memo; do not map missing places as safe.
- **Service reliability:** 2024 functionality assessment covers sampled declared villages; urban utilities and undeclared villages need separate evidence.
- **Health:** no dated local potability advisory or case surveillance assessed here.
- **Economy:** crop estimates and prices exist, but no attributed 2026 water-loss series has been built.
- **Ecology:** environmental-flow and fisheries evidence still unverified.
- **Inequality:** national rural/urban access is supported for 2018; current class, disability, migrant, settlement and sex-disaggregated outcomes remain open.

## Publication disposition

EA-01–04 can support **carefully worded national explainers** with prominent observation dates and denominators. EA-05–08 support methods and investigation paths; they do not establish a 2026 crisis or realized loss. A dependency diagram and evidence matrix are appropriate visual candidates. A household cost calculator would require locally sourced inputs and an explicit “illustrative, not observed” label. Human editorial and source review is still required before public copy, and practical health guidance needs competent-authority linkage and human review.
