### Narrow proposed public claims, subject to independent copy and source review

1. **Observed/reported, cohort limited:** “In its **10 September 2026** bulletin, the Central Water Commission reported **130.554 billion cubic metres (BCM)** of live storage across **178 monitored reservoirs**, or **70.66%** of their combined **184.762 BCM live capacity at full reservoir level**.” The source says this monitored capacity is about **71.67%** of estimated nationally created live capacity (**257.812 BCM**); it is not every reservoir, all usable water, or household supply. Locator: brief note p. 1 and basin Table p. 10. Independent arithmetic on printed totals: `100 × 130.554 / 184.762 = 70.6606%`.
2. **Regional contrast, same bulletin:** “CWC's **southern region**—its specified monitored reservoirs in Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, Kerala, Tamil Nadu and Telangana—had **30.467 BCM** in **50 reservoirs**, **54.52%** of their combined **55.879 BCM** live capacity on 10 September. The bulletin reported their storage below both the corresponding prior-year and ten-year normal values, while its eastern, western and central regional totals were above their normal values.” Locator: brief note pp. 1–2 and Table 01. Calculation: `100 × 30.467 / 55.879 = 54.5232%`. This CWC “southern region” is **not** the IMD South Peninsular rainfall region; never merge their boundaries or periods.
3. **Basin context, source-defined inventory:** “For reservoirs CWC groups under the **Cauvery** basin, the 10 September bulletin reported **3.220 BCM** of current live storage against **6.265 BCM** live capacity and **4.795 BCM** ten-year normal storage for that week. Its **Pennar** group had **0.434 BCM** against **2.475 BCM** capacity and **1.333 BCM** normal storage.” Locator: basin table, printed p. 10. Arithmetic: Cauvery fill `51.40%`, departure from normal `100 × (3.220 / 4.795 − 1) = −32.85%`; Pennar fill `17.54%`, departure `−67.44%` at displayed precision. CWC classifies Cauvery “deficient” and Pennar “highly deficient” **within this monitored-reservoir normal-storage scheme**, not as official drought declarations or whole-basin water availability. Its definitions are in printed pp. 3–5. Basin assignments, missing reservoirs and catchment-to-supply links are not independently audited.
4. **Source-defined normal, bounded:** “CWC defines its ‘normal storage’ as the average storage of the previous ten years.” Locator: brief note printed p. 3 / p. 5. Do not infer an exact fixed climatological decade without obtaining CWC's year list and historical cohort method. The 10 September national total is **93.07% of reported normal** (`130.554 / 140.271`), which is distinct from **70.66% of capacity**. The source's 2025 corresponding-week total (**163.438 BCM**) is printed for the 178-reservoir bulletin, but a reservoir-ID and capacity crosswalk is still needed before a stronger year-on-year trend claim.

The [3 September bulletin](https://rsms.cwc.gov.in/admin/storage/bulletins/bulletin-03-09-2026-118.pdf) also names 178 monitored reservoirs with **184.762 BCM** combined capacity and **129.870 BCM** current live storage. A one-week difference of **+0.684 BCM** on printed national totals is arithmetically correct, but hold any trend framing until reservoir IDs, revisions and reporting completeness are compared. The PDF content table still says “166 important reservoirs” for an appendix while the cover, main note and main tables say **178**; this appears to be an uncorrected heading and needs an explicit data-quality note.

### Reproducibility and limits

Use `pdftotext -layout` to inspect text, then visually verify the rendered pages and table cells. Preserve the source PDF hash and individual basin/reservoir rows with the bulletin date. Recompute **fill** from summed current live storage / summed live capacity within a fixed cohort. Recompute **departure from normal** from current / same-date normal, never from percent fill. The bulletin's country total (130.554 BCM) equals its basin-table total; the five regional current-storage subtotals in the brief note sum to **130.554 BCM**. `10 Sep` status precedes the IMD 23 September rainfall snapshot by 13 days. It cannot explain 23 September household conditions. Regional/basin totals represent *monitored storage*, not river discharge, aquifer stocks, delivered supply or potable volume. Reservoir operating rules, dead storage, allocations, inflows and quality are absent. A map or district inference would require verified reservoir geographies, basin crosswalk, map rights and service-area links.

**Rights:** The RSMS public page footer states “Copyright © 2020 - All Rights Reserved” for CWC. I found no explicit open redistribution licence for the bulletin. Link to the original PDF and use brief attributed facts/independent calculations; do not mirror the PDF or wholesale tables in the public build until rights are cleared. Candidate PDF copies remain local and ignored.
