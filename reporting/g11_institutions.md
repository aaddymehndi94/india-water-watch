I parsed all three original annexures by row and severity: **101 + 23 + 53 = 177 listed district/taluk pairs**, comprising **161 severe and 16 moderate**. A normalized exact-label comparison found **no duplicate district/taluk pairs** between the three documents. This is an **administrative status count as of 22 September**, not a share of India, a household shortage measure, or a crop-loss count. Spellings differ across notices (for example, *Hassan/Hasan* and *Vijayanagar/Vijayanagara*), so a publication map needs a separately verified taluk boundary/code crosswalk. The orders' own “additional” wording and independent annexure check support the summed count.

## Relief and funding stage check

The gazettes establish **notification and authorization**, not a rupee sanction, transfer or delivery. I searched official Karnataka disaster/revenue sources and central releases through **24 September 2026** for the drought memorandum, financial orders, bank transfers, tanker/service logs and crop-survey results. **No original 2026 Karnataka drought disbursement or verified beneficiary-service record was retrieved.** This is a reporting gap, not evidence that no aid occurred. News reports of a **₹3,705.30 crore state request** and of district drinking-water allocations are leads only: the original signed letter/memorandum and treasury release records were not located, so do not present their amounts as verified disbursement or put them in a chart.

The [Ministry of Home Affairs' 4 August 2026 Lok Sabha reply, PIB release 2294241](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2294241&lang=1&reg=1), opening paragraphs and Annexure II, says state governments are responsible for relief on the ground, using SDRF and other resources; additional NDRF assistance for severe disasters follows inter-ministerial assessment and procedure. The annexed state funding figures are explicitly **FY 2023–24 to 2025–26, for all calamities**. Karnataka's FY 2025–26 SDRF central release and NDRF figure in that table must **not** be used as 2026 kharif drought money. HTML snapshot SHA-256 `180c2b0075c22f051a9fddd806b293ca8d4f5b59cf092a45d5b0fb36b4aafb15`.

An [August 2026 MHA announcement on SDRF advances](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2293022&lang=1&reg=1) is explicitly for **flood-affected states** and distinguishes approval from completed payment; it is not a Karnataka drought response record. Keeping flood and drought disbursements separate matters for the India-wide story.

## Current national preparation and implementation, with limits

- The [Prime Minister's Office 7 July review, PIB release 2282256](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2282256&lang=1&reg=3), body paragraphs 5–14, reported weekly Crop Weather Watch meetings and **updated agriculture contingency plans for 262 vulnerable districts**. The Drinking Water and Sanitation Department described its monitored situation as **stable at that date**; water-resource officials likewise described reservoir/groundwater status then as stable. That is dated counterevidence to a claim of a July nationwide supply emergency, but cannot establish September conditions. Snapshot SHA-256 `2d783e86f906793e0942653baa393799a244ce7cefaa0737f5eae5bf9075eedc`.
- A [23 June Agriculture Ministry release, PIB release 2277178](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2277178&lang=1&reg=1) described **315 districts potentially affected** by weak monsoon conditions and directions on local plans, water conservation and alternate crops. The **315 potential districts** and **262 districts with updated plans** are different concepts and must not be drawn as a shrinking drought count. Its sentence says “12 states” but lists 11, another reason to request the roster before mapping. Prior source snapshot and detail: `reporting/new_response_depth.md`.
- The [Ministry of Rural Development's 1 September WDC-PMKSY 2.0 review, PIB release 2305550](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=2305550&lang=1&reg=3), body paragraphs 3–9, reports **1,220 sanctioned watershed projects**, a **₹12,404 crore sanctioned cost**, **₹8,134 crore central share**, and **₹6,955.75 crore central share released** cumulatively under that programme. It separately **reports** construction/rejuvenation of **1.24 lakh water-harvesting structures** and additional protective irrigation in **3.08 lakh hectares**; these are ministry-reported implementation/outcome metrics, not an independent evaluation of 2026 drought relief or a service measure for Karnataka's declared taluks. Snapshot SHA-256 `48069c0d75f2381e3b32e78af9d8542f08fd496856af13b98768fc7d001470ad`. If used, verify project geography, cohort, dates and source methodology before turning outputs into an effectiveness claim.

## What can be said about next steps

The [Government of India *Manual for Drought Management* (2016, updated December 2020)](https://vedas.sac.gov.in/static/pdf/Drought%20Manual-2020.pdf), §3.4, and the three gazettes document a process of continuing assessment, further notifications if criteria are met, state relief under SDRF norms, a joint crop-loss survey and possible central assessment for additional NDRF assistance. **These are prescribed or stated steps, not a prediction** that more taluks will qualify, that a given amount will be released, or that a household will receive water. A public response tracker should have separate rows for **assessment → declaration → amount sanctioned → amount released → district/beneficiary delivery → measured result** and mark later stages **unknown** until documents arrive.

**Next source tasks:** obtain the original 10 September state drought memorandum and 24 September letter, if published; obtain state treasury orders and district spending/service logs; reconcile drought-area boundary codes; ask the Agriculture Department for the joint crop-loss survey and implementation dates; and record any later changes to the gazettes. Do not infer completion from an order's authorization language.
