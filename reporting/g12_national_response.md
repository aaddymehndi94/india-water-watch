## Government mechanism versus 2026 action

[MHA’s 4 August 2026 Parliament answer](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2294241&lang=1&reg=1) says state governments bear primary responsibility for ground-level relief under national disaster policy; the Centre supplements them, and additional NDRF assistance for severe disasters follows inter-ministerial assessment and prescribed procedure. This explains what **may** follow a declaration or flood assessment; it is not a promise that a specific 2026 drought district will receive funds. G11 already records its older-year, all-calamity financial tables and warns against assigning them to current drought.

## Contrary evidence and gaps

- These flood records are explicit counterevidence to a uniformly dry, uniformly water-short 2026 India story. They do not disprove local drought declarations in Karnataka; floods and agricultural drought can occur in different places or times.
- Searched official MHA/PIB, Odisha CM, Bihar state and IMD/CWC/NDMA result surfaces for dated 2026 orders and advisories. A Bihar state DBT order and transaction summary were not located in this bounded pass; the Bihar WRD portal indexed a 2026 flood-control order but returned a gateway error when opened. Search results from broadcasters and secondary press, including Maharashtra drought announcements, are leads only, not formal notification evidence.
- This memo does not establish live warning status on 24 September. Forecast and flash-flood alerts expire rapidly; an old IMD press release is not an active alert. Check issue time, valid window and current official warning page before publication.
- No recipient interview, independent audit, aggregate rescue outcome, or full relief-disbursement ledger was obtained. Attributions should remain visible in any public timeline. Do not rate government effectiveness from these entries.
