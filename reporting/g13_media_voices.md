# G13 media and voices desk — candidate material

Audited 24 September 2026 (IST). Desk research only; no interview was conducted by India Water Watch. This is a bounded discovery inventory, **not** approval to promote every reported assertion to our claim ledger. Newspaper and broadcast reports are attributed secondary evidence. Rainfall, reservoir, policy and financial numbers should be replayed against their primary providers before use as our own figures.

## Finding: the premise of media silence does not hold

This non-exhaustive check located coverage from national outlets (The Indian Express, Hindustan Times, India Today), regional outlets (Telangana Today), a Marathi newsroom (Sakal), and AP. It includes farmer interviews, ministerial statements, protests, crop and tanker reporting, and counterevidence from heavy rain/flood coverage. A fair public framing is: **the story is being reported, often locally; the difficult task is joining sharply different conditions and testing whether reported promises became action.** We have not measured the share of TV airtime, audience reach, or story prominence, so no claim about “why channels are silent” or media motives is supportable. Repeated pickup of a wire report must count as one source chain.

## Visual treatment that can ship now

Build a floating **original typographic press collage**: cards use our own concise paraphrased finding, date, place, category (“Reported voice”, “Official statement”, “Local reporting”, “Counterevidence”), publisher name in plain type and link to the article. Do not mimic mastheads/logos or reproduce article screenshots, photos, drawings, full headlines, layouts or large excerpts. On hover/focus, card expands to show one short, carefully attributed quote where permitted, plus “Open original report”. Motion should pause for reduced-motion preference and permit keyboard navigation. Float/rotate only card containers; retain readable text orientation and contrast. Include one wet/recovery card so the collage does not fabricate a uniform national condition. A local-language card can present a short Marathi excerpt and verified translation side by side.

If photography is essential, source a 2026 image with **explicit** license to redistribute on this site (or commission and obtain written consent). The examined newspaper/agency images did not carry such permission; several were PTI/ANI/AP or outlet art, and one Lok­satta visual disclosed AI graphics. Article links can let readers see the originals on their publishers’ pages. A photo of a past drought must be dated and captioned as historical, never passed off as a 2026 scene. Generated imagery must be marked illustration and cannot depict a real interviewed person/event.

## Risks and follow-up

- **Quote limits:** keep any excerpt short, link to the source and credit the *original outlet and reporter*. The candidate extracts above are intentionally minimal. Avoid serial extracts from one article. The HT [terms](https://www.hindustantimes.com/termsofuse) explicitly reserve its content and images; AP [licenses photos separately](https://www.ap.org/content/formats/photos/). This memo does not claim a fair-use determination or grant a sublicense.
- **Political and source-chain discipline:** Fadnavis’s promised GR is not proof of a formal order; each release/delivery stage needs documentary support. His “worse than 1972” remark must not become the site’s headline without matched research. Claims about obstructed farmer access in M05 need independent corroboration and right of reply before publication.
- **Multiple kinds of water stress:** Sangareddy’s near-normal rainfall with low inflows, Marathwada’s mixed dam storage, Telangana’s late heavy showers, Lucknow’s recovery, and Assam floods all complicate a simple national shortage narrative. Display observed scopes and dates prominently.
- **Next desk action:** lead should choose 4–6 cards for the design worker, verify quote translations and primary numbers, and run independent fact/rights review. Only then promote precise claims to the public ledger. If original 2026 photos are desired, obtain rights or explicitly licensed assets before inclusion.
