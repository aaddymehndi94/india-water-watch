## The response chain to report for each place

Record six separate dated fields: **public announcement → actual state notification/order → sanction or budget allocation → treasury release → implementation evidence → measured outcome**. Preserve an original order number and jurisdiction. Report an empty field as “not verified,” not “none.” Ask which state department and local utility owns drinking-water supply, which agriculture/revenue department assesses drought, whether a State Executive Committee assessed emergency needs, and whether a central team or fund decision followed. The MHA rules above describe a process; they do not prove any 2026 state completed it. The central JJM programme also should not be presented as proof of drought relief.

## Outlook and conditional scenario language

The defensible public phrasing is: “IMD forecast below-normal all-India September rainfall on 31 August; observed rainfall, storage, groundwater, utility service and local declarations must be checked independently.” The [IMD release index](https://internal.imd.gov.in/pages/press_release_mausam.php) lists a 2026-09-17 extended-range bulletin for 17–30 September (entry 5323); the PDF timed out during this desk review, so its detailed claims remain **unverified**. IMD says extended-range forecasts update weekly, making an older bulletin unsafe as a standing forecast. Recheck the issue time and horizon whenever republishing.

An **illustrative scenario**, not a prediction: repeated low catchment rain plus unrecovered reservoir/groundwater levels and high withdrawals could constrain a specific supply system. Test each link with that system's source map, usable storage, operating orders, demand and service logs. A recovery branch is sustained catchment replenishment, improved usable storage and verified service restoration. Rainfall or a full-looking reservoir alone cannot establish reliable household water or safe quality. Do not assign a probability, national “day zero,” crop loss, mortality or political action to this scenario.

## Reader and journalist desk, by evidence type

- **Current official weather and agriculture information:** [IMD forecasts and warnings](https://mausam.imd.gov.in/) and [district agromet bulletins in local languages](https://mausam.imd.gov.in/imd_latest/contents/agromet/advisory/blocalmain.php). Check a bulletin's issue date and crop/place before translating or summarising it. This is an official public statement, not reporting by this project.
- **Water quality:** [JJM water-quality monitoring framework](https://jaljeevanmission.gov.in/sites/default/files/guideline/WQMS-Framework.pdf), §9, describes laboratory and field-test reporting; a dashboard test is a sampled result, not a guarantee for every tap. For flood-related contamination, [NCDC's public-health advisory](https://ncdc.mohfw.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/1_NPCCHH_Public-Health-Advisory_Floods_090822.pdf), p. 8, tells readers to consult local authorities on whether tap water is safe. Link to the operative local advisory before giving place-specific action. Avoid chemical dosing or clinical instructions.
- **Household/community:** Report leaks and supply disruption to the verified local utility or panchayat channel; follow current local supply/quality notices; document dates, area and response for collective escalation. A national contact number has **not** been verified for this memo. No product recommendations or assumption that every household can store water.
- **Farm:** Use the current district crop advisory and local agriculture office for crop-stage-specific decisions. Do not infer a crop loss from a rain map. [IMD's district bulletins](https://mausam.imd.gov.in/responsive/agromet_adv_ser_district_current_en.php) are a dated source route, not universal advice.
- **Journalist document requests (draft only):** Ask the state authority for the drought assessment, village verification and notification; ask the utility for source-wise supply, interruptions and quality tests; ask the treasury for sanction/release/utilisation; ask for contracts, tanker logs and completion reports where lawfully public; compare promised service with the [JJM functionality assessment](https://jaljeevanmission.gov.in/functionality-report-2024). No request has been sent. Do not imply misconduct from a missing online file; allow right of reply before sensitive allegations.

## Recovery and counterevidence to track

Use dated paired indicators: IMD catchment rain and seasonal cutoff; CWC reservoir **usable** storage with stable cohort and same date; groundwater measurements with well coverage/lag; local utility hours, volume and quality; repair completion records; independent functionality sampling. A release claiming a repaired scheme is **reported implementation** until a completion record and service evidence establish more. Improved rain can explain some replenishment without validating a programme's claimed causal effect. The 2024 functionality assessments provide a countercheck on connection-only narratives, but cannot describe September 2026 service.
