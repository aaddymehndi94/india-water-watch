## Publication and rights limits

The three newspaper excerpts are short, exact and tied to named original publications; use each once in a contextual, dated editorial card. These are **reported voices**, not India Water Watch interviews. No blanket permission to reuse publisher photographs, graphic layouts, article screenshots, logos or long passages follows from linking or a short quotation. TNIE, Mongabay and Indian Express are three distinct reporting chains; republications of their own stories would not count as independent corroboration of a speaker's losses or a policy outcome.

The district QA and official CFPI claims are best used as **method and market context**, respectively. Neither joins to the voices as a causal measurement. An August price-index rate, a September rainfall departure, an administrative drought declaration and one household/farm account have different periods, geographies, denominators and evidentiary status.

**Checks actually run:** reopened TNIE, Mongabay and Indian Express originals; confirmed exact quote fragments, speakers, bylines, article dates and local contexts; direct verified-HTTPS retrieval and independent JSON row/date/missingness/arithmetic audit of the IMD page; direct verified-HTTPS PIB release read of August CFPI table; official Maharashtra title check. No field interview, payment record, independent crop-loss survey, image licence, or legal opinion was obtained. The proposed claims may enter a candidate ledger with these limits and need one final check against the exact public rendering before release.
