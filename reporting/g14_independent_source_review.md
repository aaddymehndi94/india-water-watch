## Cross-source constraints and corrections

- **No false currentness:** 24 September is the rainfall observation label on two mutable IMD GIFs. It is neither the time IMD published the files nor the time this project reviewed/deployed them. Preserve retrieval hash/time and editorial review time separately. The G14 local-data report's source SHA values matched my independent fetches exactly.
- **No synthetic national conclusion:** The four region departures cannot be averaged to reproduce national −14.2%; without region totals and weighting that is an invalid operation. A rainfall deficit by itself does not determine agricultural loss, usable supply, price changes or stock returns.
- **No false funding stage:** The letter is an original political statement and public request. News reports repeating that letter are one evidence chain, not independent evidence of payment. The Gazette's declaration-stage “sanction” must never be presented as a sanction of the ₹3,705.30 crore request.
- **No imagery laundering:** News photographs, publisher logos and article screenshots remain on hold without specific republication rights. An original-text press collage can link to the reports. The Commons flood photograph must not be used to dramatize drought in another place or month.
- **Political title check:** Current Karnataka government pages and contemporaneous reporting identify D.K. Shivakumar as Chief Minister in September 2026, consistent with the signed letter. Older cached biographical pages may still call him Deputy Chief Minister and are stale for this date.
