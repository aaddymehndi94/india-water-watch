## Rendered and rights checks

- In **both** candidate builds, static HTML contains the 119.6 mm explanation, all three excerpts with dates/outlet attribution and source-record links, the MoSPI month/base/cause caveat, the district row QA counts and the response-stage caveat. The prefix build voice links include `/india-water-watch/` as expected. The five new source pages exist and each links to its original URL.
- The three excerpts are brief and directly attributed; the homepage labels them curated published reporting, not India Water Watch interviews. There is **no outlet photo, masthead, screenshot, facsimile or article layout** in the bundled raster assets. I found only the pre-existing abstract hero image and the separately reviewed, credited Commons July Nashik flood image in the root build. This review supports the narrow excerpt reuse in context, not blanket reuse of outlet media or an assurance against all legal claims.
- The three outlet stories are separate reporting chains; the political pledge's single article is **one reported source**, not independent official corroboration. The official MoSPI and IMD products are distinct providers. The homepage's contrast between deficit and local flooding is presented as differing geographies and experiences, not an internally inconsistent national measurement.

## Method and remaining limits

I checked the five promoted evidence records against their saved files: all five `local_snapshot_path` files exist and their SHA-256 hashes match the approved ledger. The IMD national graphic hash also matches its approved record. I recomputed the rainfall and district counts with independent short Python reads and inspected built HTML with Beautiful Soup; I did **not** run the site's full build, browser, visual, accessibility or security suites as part of this read-only source assignment. The older G14 source/rights conclusions and known 23/24 September asynchronous product-date limit are documented in `reporting/g14_independent_source_review.md` and `reporting/g14_postpromotion_source_review.md`.
