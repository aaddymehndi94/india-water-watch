## Economic and market transmission: evidence, not causation theatre

1. **Observed price context:** [MoSPI's 14 Sep release for August 2026, CPI base 2024=100](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2310058&lang=1&reg=1) reports provisional **5.95% year-on-year Consumer Food Price Index inflation** (rural 6.13%, urban 5.64%) and **4.82% headline CPI**. July's final food inflation in the same release is 5.52%. These are nationwide price changes, not a measured rainfall effect. August price data precede the late-September drought declarations; do not label the change “caused by 2026 water stress.”
2. **Plausible supply channel with counterweight:** [Reuters reporting published 17 Sep 2026](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/india-rice-output-faces-biggest-drop-in-two-decades-stocks-cushion-exports-ce785bd3db88fe23) quotes Rice Exporters Association president B.V. Krishna Rao's **forecast** of a roughly 10 million tonne production drop; it also reports government data showing summer rice planted area 42.68 million hectares as of 11 Sep, about 4% lower year-on-year, and large rice reserves. The production drop is an **industry forecast**, not a realized crop estimate. The reserves are an important buffer and argue against a claim of automatic shortage or export collapse. Obtain the original Agriculture Ministry area series and FCI stocks before making the planted-area/stock figures standalone project observations; Reuters syndication alone is a lead.
3. **Equity mechanism:** Reddy's reported loan/failed crop, Aishah Khan's time cost, and Reuters's stock buffer illustrate different exposures and coping capacities. They are **examples**, not a representative household distribution. A wealthy person's buffer and a low-income person's harm cannot be quantified from these accounts.
4. **Stock markets:** No checked 2026 company earnings release, exchange filing, or causal event study establishes a rainfall-driven stock move. A rainfall → crop → price → company revenue or input cost → earnings → stock path remains a **conditional exposure chain**. Do not attach a price ticker to a deficit map or claim “the monsoon hit the Sensex.” An auditable future market card needs a named issuer's dated disclosure, relevant water footprint, observed operational impact, event timing and competing explanations.

## Reuse and design guardrails

- Use **one short attributed excerpt** per publisher story at most, with original link and article date. For AP June, preferably paraphrase both voices so aggregate use stays clearly limited. The quoted phrases above are source-identification excerpts, not a license to republish the stories.
- Create original typographic source cards that say **Reported by AP / TNIE / Mongabay / Indian Express**. Do not make a facsimile newspaper page, scrape logos, crop outlet photos, or use screenshots without permission. Source stories and article photos remain publisher copyrighted. Link out for full context.
- Display dates on every voice card. Clearly label direct quotations versus reporter paraphrases, and label politician remarks as **statement**, **request** or **pledge**. Do not equate a pledge with delivery.
- State that this is a curated sample of reporting, not a census of who is speaking. The examined AP, TNIE, Mongabay, Reuters, Indian Express and local outlets demonstrate active coverage; there is no basis for “news channels are not reporting this.”
