## Review of choices and changes

`reporting/g13_decision_review.md` records the candid design audit. The Astro/React/TypeScript/Tailwind stack was capable, but the previous opening delayed the strongest verified reading, repeated cards and offered little control. The visual news revision moves the completed June historical rank and the separate season-to-date rainfall reading onto the first mobile screen. Its original symbolic artwork is labeled; it is not a satellite photograph or a map. A reader-controlled rainfall stage retains exact dates, values, units and sources, including a no-JavaScript reading. A 36-place schematic locator improves access to the state/UT atlas without inventing measured state values.

The new G13b chapter is a five-card floating **typographic press mosaic** linking Nagpur, Marathwada, Sangareddy, Jurala and Lucknow reports. It shows a farmer's reported experience, an official allocation position, contrasting reservoir/rainfall conditions and a recovery counter-signal. Each card gives its outlet, place and report date. These are original short summaries of other outlets' reporting, not interviews or republished front pages. Outlet photographs, agency images, logos and article screenshots were withheld because reuse rights were not established. The independent media audit rejected the claim that news organizations are silent: substantial local and national reporting exists.

An interactive **water-to-market evidence ladder** now asks readers to open five causal gates: water conditions, company exposure, operations, earnings and share price. JSW Steel's FY2025–26 annual report discloses water availability as a production risk, while its FY2024–25 report says no water-related incident *materially* affected costs or revenues in the preceding four fiscal years. A separate historic CEA inset records 24 raw-water-related thermal outages and 2,875.79 MU of potential generation loss in FY2016–17. The 2026 operations, earnings and stock-price gates are marked **not verified here**. The national rainfall number is expressly not a measurement at a JSW plant. This interaction does not offer an investment prediction.

History presents IMD's **sixth-lowest June since 1901** without ranking the ongoing full monsoon. Impacts presents two discrete DCA Bengaluru onion-centre prices, ₹23/kg on 23 September 2025 and ₹57/kg on 23 September 2026, with no weather-causation assertion. Response distinguishes Karnataka's 177 declared taluks from a separate July flood rescue and August flood-funding approval. A labeled low-confidence AI Author scenario remains conditional, with assumptions and reversal signals. No national household-shortage count or water-driven stock move is claimed.
