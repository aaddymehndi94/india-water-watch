# G15 design and editorial review · 24 September 2026

## Verdict on the earlier site

The Astro static architecture is adequate for a visual feature: it supports readable prerendered chapters, React interactions, Tailwind utilities, D3 charts, Pagefind search and portable builds. The weakness was the public presentation. Earlier iterations opened with a cautious case-study rhythm, placed too many similarly weighted cards in sequence, and made readers scroll before reaching an interactive comparison. The state/UT locator was deliberately schematic because no reusable, versioned boundary file was cleared; that kept it honest but did not satisfy an expectation of a geographic data map. A cumulative national rainfall departure was present, but its scale and gap were not felt visually. The response chapter made readers work too hard to distinguish a request from aid delivered. Attribution existed in source records, but named people and their distinct experiences did not carry enough visual weight.

## Decisions in this revision

- Put a large, shared-zero rainfall comparison immediately after the opening. The hatched 119.6 mm gap is computed from the same-dated IMD values, 843.2 and 723.6 mm through 24 September. The control isolates the gap without interpolating daily rain. The older June comparison remains available in a disclosure because June and June–September are different windows.
- Give a farmer account, a flood-affected resident account and an official pledge an immersive three-panel scene. Each panel retains the speaker, reporting outlet, exact date, location, source link, claim ID and limit. The interaction has URL state and keyboard support; all three accounts are present without JavaScript. The words are short, attributed quotations from published reports, not interviews by this publication.
- Make the response page open with the difference between 177 declared Karnataka taluks and the requested ₹1,852.65 crore. The latter is a request; sanction, transfer, delivery and outcome remain separately unverified. The existing six-stage tracker supplies the deeper flow.
- Show the district map gap as a graphic of the actual IMD page audit: 722 row-dated numeric entries, eight older numeric entries and 31 explicit No Data entries among 761 unique map areas. Two displayed departures differ materially from recomputation using the displayed millimetres. The page explains why these are source-page quality findings, not approved district water observations.
- Add August's provisional national food-price context to the economy chapter with its official MoSPI date and definition. The 5.95% CFPI year-on-year rate is not presented as a water-caused price effect or stock-market signal.

## Where restraint improves the feature

The press sample shows that outlets have reported farm, flood and allocation stories, so a claim that newsrooms are silent would mislead readers. Publisher article screenshots and photographs are not used without reuse rights; a licensed July Nashik flood photograph elsewhere is dated and credited and never passed off as September drought imagery. A 2024 Karnataka water-service survey was independently checked but its reuse terms and different period prevent promotion as a 2026 district picture. The incomplete 2026 monsoon has no defensible full-season historical rank. No verified 2026 water-attributed stock move, nationwide household-shortage count or next-monsoon rainfall forecast is known. Graphic force must come from the verified distinction between what is measured, reported and still unknown.

## Next design opportunities tied to new evidence

A genuine district map needs row-level date/arithmetic repair, a stable boundary crosswalk and geometry rights. A reservoir animation needs two matched CWC cohort dates; the currently linked newer official PDF was unavailable. A market movement graphic needs a named event, dated operational disclosure and a defensible attribution method. A household impact scene needs current local service and quality records or permissioned original reporting. Each would be stronger than animating inferred measurements or using stock footage as an observed 2026 scene.

The G15 work is a publication candidate until exact public-copy source review, accessibility/security review, final-build verification and live deployment are recorded.
