## Editorial and visual observations

- The press section is an **original typographic collage**. Its five cards are clearly marked with place, publisher and report date, link to the original stories, and state that this project did not interview named people. No outlet photograph, masthead, screenshot or article body appears in the published collage. The recovery card counters a one-directional national shortage impression. The legal status of any future copied photograph remains **not cleared**.
- The market ladder uses active controls, a clear five-step causation question and separate source links. Its first national rainfall gate explicitly says the value cannot be assigned to JSW facilities. Its company disclosure is a different date and record. Operations, earnings and share-price gates say **not verified here**; the older CEA outage count is in a separate archive panel, not a 2026 stock event. The independent source/methodology review should remain the authority for those numerical and factual statements.
- At 360 and 390 pixels, the hero numbers and both press-card and market text remain legible without page overflow. At 768 and 1440, the press cards have distinct scale and spacing. Light and dark render without contrast violations in axe, but automated color checks are not a complete manual WCAG audit. The hero image is set to eager load and async decode; on a cold automated screenshot taken before decode its dark fallback can briefly appear. After explicit image decode the intended visible water image appears at both mobile widths. The fallback remains readable and truthful.
