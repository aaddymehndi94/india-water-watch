# G14 independent visual, accessibility, security and rights review

**Reviewed snapshot:** frozen 24 September 2026 root `dist/` and GitHub project-prefix `dist-github/` after the G14 contrast correction and the 16:04 IST progress-metadata rebuild. ## Verdict

**Passed for the tested local G14 release candidate.** No remaining material visual, interaction, automated accessibility, route, public-output or photo-attribution failure was found. The site keeps the 23 and 24 September rainfall readings distinct, and the response feature does not present a request as a payment or a July flood photograph as September drought evidence. Release metadata and source/methodology accuracy remain the lead and independent source reviewer's responsibility.

## Defects found and resolved before this verdict

1. The first G14 build gave the new press-collage link to the licensed July photograph a pale cyan foreground on a light green background. Axe measured **1.16:1** contrast and marked it serious at 360 and 1440 pixels. I reported the exact selector (`a[href$="response/#flood-photo"]`); the lead changed the link to dark teal. The final root and prefix axe runs below have **zero violations**.
2. The response page briefly referred to the “23 September rainfall graphic” while the current featured graphic had advanced to 24 September. The lead corrected the wording before the reviewed build. The 23 September observation remains available as an explicitly dated earlier snapshot, and the AI Author assessment retains its original issue-time 23 September evidence link rather than silently refreshing the assessment.
3. A 360-pixel Karnataka assistance request card initially overflowed. The lead fixed it before freeze. The final browser test found no page overflow on response at 360; direct inspection of `prelim-request-360.png` showed the amount, request status and limitation readable.

## Visual inspection

I inspected the actual rendered mobile and desktop home, Explore and response screenshots in both themes, plus the 360/1440-pixel rainfall workbench and the 390-pixel documentary photo and response flow. Rainfall snapshot controls, bars, units, period, source action and the selected value remain readable on mobile; the chart is a dated departure view with a fixed axis, not a severity map. The photograph's date and full credit remain legible below the image on mobile. The six-stage panel keeps its distinction among documented actions, directed survey and later unverified outcomes. The home image and two dated rainfall facts remain the stronger entry point; interior pages retain more conventional article headers, which is a design limitation rather than a release-blocking defect in this snapshot.

## Limits and next action

These tests do not establish full WCAG conformance, screen-reader quality, 200% zoom behavior, mobile-network performance, actual reader comprehension, outside publisher-link availability or legal chain of title beyond the Commons/sidecar evidence. Axe can miss contrast over images and semantic problems. I did not independently recompute the IMD/CEA/government figures or perform human editorial review; the separate G14 source review addresses factual support. If public code, approved data, the licensed file or release output changes after this snapshot, rerun affected checks before deploying. Retain the complete linked credit and July date in future photo edits and portable ZIPs.
