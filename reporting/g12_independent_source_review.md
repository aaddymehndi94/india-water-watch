## Attempted disproof and material caveats

**IMD series version:** The June PDF p. 1 reports 2026 as *103 mm* after rounding, while its p. 3 table prints *102.5 mm*. These are compatible. Its historic five lower totals differ from the mutable IMD monthly HTML (`2014 92.8` in PDF versus `92.1` in HTML, for example), so the HTML arithmetic is a consistency check on the identities/rank, **not** a merged numerical series. The memo now correctly derives the sixth-lowest and “last lower in 2014” sentence from the same PDF. Neither product has a matched daily 23 Sep historical table. The PDF's June East/Northeast rank is likewise a separate completed-month statement.

**Price versus water harm:** The DCA 2025/2026 values are official *reported price entries* for the same named centre, which is stronger than a vague market anecdote. The centrewise result abbreviates its column to `Price`; the exact retail interpretation is supported by the DCA home page's matching all-India average on 23 Sep. Neither page discloses the centre sample/transaction volume. Weather, stock, arrivals, trade, logistics and other market factors could move onion prices. The current rice/pulse sowing report is a different crop and earlier date. Putting these figures side by side requires a caption that explicitly rejects a proven causal chain.

**Government stages:** The MHA flood advance is an approval. Gujarat rescue is an agency-reported carried-out activity. Odisha is a visit/direction. Bihar DBT is a minister-reported start, not audited receipt. These are one government account each, not multiple independent witnesses; PIB republishes the issuing ministry's own content. Flood response in some states is strong counterevidence to a uniform national drought narrative and does not invalidate Karnataka's separately gazetted drought declarations.

**Map rights/portrayal:** The geoBoundaries record-specific CC BY 2.5 India licence could support reuse with attribution *if its geometry were fit to publish*. Structural roster matching does not resolve its `2011` represented-year field, modern administrative unit dates, or border alignment. SoI guidance on free digital display does not by itself resolve its separate reproduction/copyright notice for downloaded ABDB material. This review does not offer legal clearance. A table/mosaic remains an accurate state selector while boundary sourcing is unresolved.

**Rights and freshness:** Use brief, original, attributed factual statements and independently drawn diagrams. Do not redistribute IMD PDF figures/long table rows, bulk DCA form results, agency photographs, SoI geometry or candidate geoBoundaries polygons in the public ZIP without source-specific rights and portrayal review. Date every observation, provider publication, retrieval and deployment separately. DCA form responses and official HTML may change after this 24 Sep retrieval; preserved hashes identify the reviewed snapshot, not a guarantee of future values.
