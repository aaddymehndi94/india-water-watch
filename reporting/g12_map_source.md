# G12 boundary-source audit — India state/UT map

**Reviewed 24 September 2026 (UTC); candidate only.** No geometry was promoted to `public/` or `data/approved/`. This audit concerns an explanatory ADM1 locator/selection map, not a state rainfall choropleth. The approved site has no state-scale observations to paint onto 36 states/UTs.

## Recommendation

**Hold a public ADM1 map layer for now.** The strongest official source is Survey of India's (SoI) 2025 Administrative Boundary Data Base (ABDB), but its current copyright policy and the dataset's own metadata require a rights determination before reproducing its geometry. An open-license geoBoundaries alternative joins all 36 site roster entries, but its political-border agreement with the SoI's 2026 official map has not been verified and its API's represented-year field says `2011` despite a 2023 source update and 36 modern administrative units. A 36-unit searchable table/mosaic is the defensible live alternative. A non-geographic illustration must not be called a map.

### Official candidate: Survey of India ABDB

- [ABDB download page](https://surveyofindia.gov.in/pages/administrative-boundary-data-base-abdb-) links the state/district/subdistrict archive and a separate metadata ZIP. The archive HEAD response was 202,524,438 bytes (~193 MiB). The metadata ZIP was fetched for local review only: 38,879 bytes; SHA-256 `1a14716b73f00fc8391f2708a7975c2f2c1ad4a4e91aafb6dce6546a039e9514`.
- The ZIP's `STATE BOUNDARY.xlsx` identifies dataset `SOI/ABDB/VECTOR/50000/2025/STATE/INDIA`, edition 2025, source temporal extent 2022–2025, publication date 6 May 2026, 1:50,000 equivalent scale and EPSG:7755 / LCC-WGS84. Its description lists some states/UTs harmonized to ORGI in 2024 and others in 2025; it does **not** claim every unit was updated in 2025. It states a Survey of India digital topographic database lineage and verified boundaries. Its legal constraints fields say `copyright` for both access and use, and point to the 2021 geospatial guidelines. The metadata was read as data; its archive contents were not executed.
- The [2021 guidelines as reproduced by SoI](https://onlinemaps.surveyofindia.gov.in/GeospatialGuidelines.aspx) say SoI political maps/digital boundaries are the standard, are to be downloadable without charge, and permit digital display and print. The [SoI website copyright policy](https://surveyofindia.gov.in/pages/copyright-policy) separately says reproduction of website material requires written permission and prominent attribution. The [older price/licensing page](https://surveyofindia.gov.in/pages/pricing-of-digital-data) still describes an Internet licence for older digital products. These are inconsistent enough that free download should not be treated as a clear right to redistribute raw or derived ABDB data. Do not accept a licence or seek permission on the publisher's behalf.
- SoI lists an [English political map, 13th edition/2026](https://surveyofindia.gov.in/pages/political-map-of-india) as the visual standard for claimed external borders. A boundary reviewer should compare any proposed ADM1 layer against it at Jammu & Kashmir/Ladakh, Arunachal Pradesh and the external outline; a source-label/dispute caveat alone cannot cure a material mismatch.

### Open candidate: geoBoundaries gBOpen India ADM1

- The [geoBoundaries API](https://www.geoboundaries.org/api/current/gbOpen/IND/ADM1/) returned boundary ID `IND-ADM1-1811400`, 36 ADM1 units, `boundaryYearRepresented: 2011`, source update 19 January 2023, upstream DataMeet maps, and a **record-specific** `Creative Commons Attribution 2.5 India` licence. The [geoBoundaries documentation](https://www.geoboundaries.org/api.html) describes the open release and the fields; the record-specific licence should govern over generic repository or API prose. [Creative Commons' licence page](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.5/in/) sets attribution requirements. Attribution in a published adaptation would need DataMeet/geoBoundaries names as applicable, direct source URL, licence URL and modification/simplification notice, without suggesting endorsement.
- The downloaded upstream simplified GeoJSON is still **5,103,747 bytes** and 116,391 vertices, too heavy as-is for a quick mobile interaction. SHA-256: `4c63fe43294a391e8f2de4e9f86f3edb60f8688275b9fee90c61fb2aa0c26061`. It has 36 Polygon/MultiPolygon features, one each for the 36 current roster entries, including separate Jammu & Kashmir and Ladakh and the merged Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu. Every `shapeISO` is unique, geometry coordinates are in valid longitude/latitude ranges, and a **manually reviewed explicit ISO-code crosswalk** covers 36/36 roster IDs. This establishes join coverage only; it does not establish current spatial accuracy, administration of claimed areas or 2026 SoI conformance.
- Source ISO suffixes include older forms `IN-CT`, `IN-OR`, `IN-UT`; the crosswalk maps them to Chhattisgarh, Odisha and Uttarakhand respectively. These are *source keys*, not fresh government authority codes. The source's diacritic names are retained alongside the site's plain-English names, rather than joined by fuzzy text. The API's `2011` field is internally awkward beside separate modern units; the accurate geometry vintage is unresolved. [DataMeet's repository](https://github.com/datameet/maps) states a generic CC BY 4.0 default, while the geoBoundaries record says CC BY 2.5 India; retain the record's source-specific licence and seek upstream lineage confirmation before publication. DataMeet's [country boundary note](https://github.com/datameet/maps/blob/master/Country/README.md) itself distinguishes several external-border constructions and licences, underscoring the need to identify the exact ADM1 lineage.
- geoBoundaries says its open maps represent each country as it would represent itself, without special treatment of disputed areas in its [simplified-download explanation](https://github.com/wmgeolab/gbWeb/blob/main/simplifiedDownloads.html). That is a project intent, not verification against the 2026 SoI map. Do not paint current water values onto claimed-but-unadministered territory simply because a polygon includes it. Do not present a disputed political outline as an unqualified observed administrative footprint.

## Promotion boundary and remaining work

1. Resolve SoI reuse terms for the specific ABDB geometry or establish that an alternative open layer actually conforms to SoI's current political standard. The 2021 guideline and copyright policy should be reviewed by the publisher's rights adviser before external publication of SoI-derived data.
2. Compare northern/eastern external borders and each ADM1 feature with the SoI 2026 political map; inspect islands, enclave/multipart UTs, topology, overlaps and gaps. Record reviewer, methods, deviations and whether any claimed territory is a separate non-statistical reference overlay. This audit has **not** made that comparison.
3. If approved, simplify with reproducible topology-preserving parameters, retain source and output SHA-256, version the boundary and crosswalk, show attribution, and test on mobile. Never turn the 36 shapes into a rainfall or water-crisis choropleth until matched state-level observations are independently approved.
4. Until then, keep the existing 36-unit table/mosaic as the state/UT selector, with the correct `no state observation` status. Do not infer state conditions from IMD's four homogeneous regions or CWC reservoir cohorts.
