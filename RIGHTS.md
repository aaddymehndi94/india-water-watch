# Rights boundary

The starter contains original briefs/code and links, not a republication license for linked material. Before any dataset, boundary, image or article is distributed, record its actual license, attribution and permitted use. Government provenance alone does not establish an open license. An accessible download is not automatically redistributable.

Choose a separate editorial-content license at publication, with explicit third-party exclusions. Do not place copied articles or restricted raw data in a public Git history. Keep private reporting notes and copyrighted working snapshots outside the public repository, in an ignored local evidence store. Commit metadata, hashes and short permitted supporting extracts only where allowed.

Do not import fonts from the starter environment. Use system fonts during prototyping; Codex may later source properly licensed webfonts from their legitimate upstream provider and document their rights. No font files are bundled in this pack.
